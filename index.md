# Welcome to the Land Use Inventory Wiki!

This wiki can serve as your aid to using and producing CMAP's Land Use Inventory. It contains two sections:

## [LUI Field Guide](./field_guide_index.md)

The Field Guide is our term for the documentation and guidelines that determine how land uses are classified. It contains definitions and descriptions of the land use codes, examples, and Q&A from past inventories.

## [LUI Training](./training_index.md)

Coming soon {: .label .label-yellow S}

The wiki also contains a repository of background information on the LUI project and process. Staff involved in producing the inventory are encouraged to use this as a refresher for their initial training sessions if any questions come up.
